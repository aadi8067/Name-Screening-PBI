Aditya, I can see the issue clearly. Both calculated columns are created successfully, but Power BI is throwing a Circular Dependency error when you apply sorting.

This happens because:
- Outstanding_Aging_Bucket depends on Outstanding_Age_Working_Days.
- Outstanding_Aging_Sort depends on Outstanding_Aging_Bucket.
- Then you sort Outstanding_Aging_Bucket by Outstanding_Aging_Sort.
Power BI detects a dependency loop.
Step 1: Correct the sorting columns

Close the error window.

    Fact_Outstanding[Outstanding_Age_Working_Days]

    4
